About Saguaro Lake

The lowest and nearest of the four Salt River lakes, Saguaro is formed by Stewart Mountain Dam below the Bulldog Cliffs. Its proximity to Mesa and Scottsdale makes it the go-to for a quick trip, with marina services, calm canyon coves, and a steady largemouth and crappie fishery.

  • Full pool1,529 ft
  • DamStewart Mountain Dam
  • River systemSalt River (SRP)
  • Main rampSaguaro Lake Marina (Saguaro Del Norte)
  • Top speciesLargemouth, yellow bass, crappie

Fish species

  • Largemouth bass — the mainstay, around coves, points, and the canyon walls.
  • Yellow bass — schooling fish that can be caught in numbers.
  • Crappie — good around brush and structure, especially spring.
  • Catfish & sunfish — strong through the warm months.

Best times & seasonal patterns

Spring (Feb–Apr)

Bass move shallow to spawn and crappie gather on structure — a prime, comfortable window close to town.

Summer (May–Sep)

Launch at first light to beat both the heat and the recreation traffic; go deeper midday. The daily score on b4u.fish flags the workable windows.

Fall & winter (Oct–Jan)

Cooling water revives the bass bite; catfish and crappie stay catchable around structure.

Getting there

From Mesa, take Bush Highway — reached from SR-87 (Beeline) or via Power Road off US-60 — to the Saguaro Lake Recreation Area turnoff. It's about 30–45 minutes northeast of Mesa. The lake is in Tonto National Forest, so a Tonto Pass or day-use fee is required.

Frequently asked questions

What is the water level at Saguaro Lake today?

Full pool is 1,529 ft. Live elevation and percent-full are on the b4u.fish dashboard, from the SRP/DWR daily report.

What fish are in Saguaro Lake?

Largemouth and yellow bass, crappie, catfish, sunfish, and some walleye.

Which boat ramp is open?

The Saguaro Del Norte launch by Saguaro Lake Marina is the main public ramp — see the dashboard for status.

How do I get there from Mesa?

Bush Highway to the Saguaro Lake Recreation Area turnoff, about 30–45 minutes northeast. Tonto pass required.
