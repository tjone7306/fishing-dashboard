    How the Bite Score works — 100 points across 6 factors

    25Barometric pressure & front — the biggest lever. A falling barometer ahead of a front fires the bite; a rising one shuts it down.
    20Moon & solunar — phase (new and full are strongest) plus whether the moon overhead/underfoot lands in fishable hours.
    15Wind — a light SW/S breeze (6–15 mph) is ideal; dead calm or a hard north wind costs points.
    15Cloud cover — overcast and low dawn/dusk light beat bright bluebird skies.
    15Water temp — how close the surface sits to the bass comfort zone (≈65–78°F).
    10Stability — a bonus for settled conditions; a recent front or big temperature swing drops it.
    85+ Drop everything · 70+ Solid bite · 55+ Workable · 35+ Tough day · under 35 Save the gas.
