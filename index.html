🎯 Lure & Color Cheat-Sheet

🌡️ Seasonal Pattern — Mid-April

Low-elevation lakes are in post-spawn / early summer transition. Expect a mix of bass guarding fry in 2–6 ft and males staged on first drop-offs.

  • Early AM: topwater / wake baits over spawn flats & cove mouths
  • Mid-day: shakey head / finesse worm on main-lake points 10–20 ft
  • Evening: move-bait bite returns — chatterbait, squarebill, spinnerbait
  • Trout stocking at Canyon/Saguaro typically wraps by end of April — bass feed on the leftovers. Swimbaits in rainbow pattern work.
